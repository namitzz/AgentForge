# HiddenInk privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

HiddenInk is a set of instructions (a skill and four commands) that Claude follows inside your own Claude conversation.

## What HiddenInk collects

Nothing. HiddenInk has no servers, no analytics, no tracking and no code of its own. It does not collect, store, sell or send your data anywhere. The author never sees what you check.

## What happens to the content you check

Text and files you share in a chat are processed by Claude, the same as anything else you share with Claude, under Anthropic's own privacy policy (https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy) and your Claude account settings. HiddenInk does not change how Anthropic handles your data.

HiddenInk is designed to reduce what you expose: it masks sensitive values in its replies and gives you a redacted safe copy to share instead of the original.

## Limits

HiddenInk's checks are rule-based and can miss things. A GREEN result means nothing was found, not that content is guaranteed safe. Carbon figures are estimates.
